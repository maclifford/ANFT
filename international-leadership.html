History & Lineage

ANFT Founder Amos Clifford, age 19, wears a crown of Yerba Buena made by a student during a seminar he is leading. Officials of the Santa Barbara County juvenile justice department invited Amos to teach a course to incarcerated teenagers. In an unprecedented move, they decided Amos could take them out of the secure institution for the day. In San Roque Canyon they explored a diverse array of herbs and learned about their traditional uses. Each student wrote an essay and illustrated a plant. The collection was published by the Santa Barbara County Office of Education as Useful and Healing Plants of the Santa Barbara Foothills. Early roots of ANFT, reaching back to 1974.

The land itself is the elder here and everywhere in our world. From our home in Prescott, Arizona, we work on land that has hosted many lives across its long existence — human and other-than-human, some now gone from the Earth — and many cultures, most recently, before those of us here now, the Yavapai and Apache peoples and their relations. We honor the land, and all whom it has held, and its infinite threads of relational connections, in which we and our work are embedded, which we live into with awe and love, and for which we are profoundly grateful.
