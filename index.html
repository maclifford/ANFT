Something in you remembers.

Time in nature quiets the tamed world — and an older, essential self begins to stir. Jack London called it the call of the wild. We call it The Forest Threshold: an awakening we witness, and from which many paths open.

We train people to guide others into relationship with the living world, and we certify the places where that happens.

This is the practice of entering relationship with a world that has its own voice. We hold the lineage: Relational Forest Therapy.

The Forest Threshold

The Forest Threshold is where the call reaches you. ANFT has stood at this threshold with thousands of people just like you. In over 70 nations spread across the globe. In the wild places; those within and those without. From this threshold, many paths open. We are standing by at whichever path you choose and will walk it with you.

The land itself is the elder here and everywhere in our world. From our home in Prescott, Arizona, we work on land that has hosted many lives across its long existence — human and other-than-human, some now gone from the Earth — and many cultures, most recently, before those of us here now, the Yavapai and Apache peoples and their relations. We honor the land, and all whom it has held, and its infinite threads of relational connections, in which we and our work are embedded, which we live into with awe and love, and for which we are profoundly grateful.
